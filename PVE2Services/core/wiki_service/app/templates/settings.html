ℹ️ How credentials are stored & why

What lives here: the admin account. Credentials are stored in the database as bcrypt hashes — never plaintext — sessions are HMAC-signed cookies, and connector secrets are encrypted at rest with Fernet.

Why no default password: a hardcoded admin/password is the first thing attackers try, so the first boot requires creating the account. Changing the password requires the current one; failed attempts are rate-limited (5 per minute, then a 5-minute block) and the counter is shared across replicas.
