ℹ️ How PVE2Services works

PVE2 Core is the data hub — it syncs machines, nodes and storage from your Proxmox cluster into a shared cache. Every other plugin builds on that data: Dash and Audit visualize/analyze it, DNS / Proxy / Wiki / Notify push it to external platforms, Power manages machine state on a schedule.

Configure Core first; the other plugins are independent of each other and of any external service until you point them at one. All admin routes require a session; connector secrets are encrypted at rest.
